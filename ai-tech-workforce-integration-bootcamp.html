Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our bespoke AI + Tech Workforce Integration Bootcamp deployment.

How is custom pricing determined for the HR Tech Bootcamp?

Because Tier 3 involves direct, bespoke software engineering—integrating your existing HRIS, payroll, and chat channels with custom AI workforce agents—pricing is scoped individually based on employee headcount, database infrastructure, and required automation depth.

During your 90-Minute Tech-Fit Audit, we evaluate your systems and deliver a transparent 3-page scope proposal. Every approved deployment must project at least 3x Year-1 ROI in recaptured HR hours and reduced attrition.

What HR technology platforms and AI solutions are deployed?

We bridge your people operations with our two-platform technical architecture:
  • eurodigital.ae (HR Operations Software): Unified cloud HRIS modules covering employee master records, leave management, automated attendance tracking, compliant payroll workflows, and digital documentation.
  • eurodigi.ai (Autonomous HR AI Agents): Fine-tuned internal AI agents—including 24/7 Level-1 employee policy bots on WhatsApp/Slack, automated applicant screening, conversational onboarding assistants, and leading attrition warning indicators.

Will these AI workforce systems replace our internal HR team?

No. Our design philosophy is HR Amplification, Not Replacement.

By automating 30% to 60% of repetitive, transactional queries (such as leave balances, handbook policies, and document retrieval), your HR team reclaims 5 to 25 hours per week. This freed capacity allows your people leaders to focus on high-value human priorities: culture building, leadership succession, strategic talent acquisition, and employee retention.

What are the prerequisites to enroll in this bootcamp?

To ensure high execution velocity and substantial ROI, qualification criteria include:
  • Completed Workforce X-Ray™ diagnostic or Workforce Acceleration Engine™ baseline on file.
  • Minimum active headcount of 25+ full-time employees.
  • An appointed internal HR tech champion to participate in testing and sprint reviews.
  • Completion of the 90-minute Tech-Fit Audit to confirm data readiness and integration feasibility.

How do you handle employee data privacy and regional legal compliance?

Security and regional regulatory compliance are foundational to our engineering architecture:
  • Full alignment with regional privacy laws, including the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45/2021), India DPDP Act, and GDPR.
  • Enterprise AES-256 encryption at rest and in transit with tenant database isolation.
  • Zero public LLM training: your internal company policies and employee communications remain 100% private to your tenant.

What is the timeline for deployment and team handover?

The standard technical integration spans 4 to 8 weeks:
  • Weeks 1–2: Architectural Mapping, Security Boundaries & HRIS Sandbox Setup.
  • Weeks 3–5: AI Agent Training on internal handbooks, Workflow Connectors & API Hook Deployment.
  • Weeks 6–7: Pilot Department Testing, Manager Training & Feedback Calibration.
  • Week 8+: Organization-wide Cutover & 30-Day Stabilization Support Guarantee.
