Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our 60-Day Done-With-You Business Acceleration Engine™ installation.

What does "Done-With-You" installation actually mean?

Unlike passive courses that leave you stranded with theoretical videos, Done-With-You (DWY) combines three structured implementation legs:
  • Leg 1 — Tactical Modules: 7 core modules containing 28 focused, bite-sized video lessons (8+ total hours) detailing exact SOPs and playbooks.
  • Leg 2 — Weekly Live Implementation Labs: 8 weekly 60–90 minute interactive Zoom sessions where consultants review your worksheets, audit workflows, and troubleshoot bottlenecks live.
  • Leg 3 — Execution Accountability: Matched peer accountability partner, homework verification, and dedicated WhatsApp priority support to guarantee steady momentum.

What are the exact 5 core business machines installed over the 60 days?

Over the 60-day installation cycle, we build and deploy five durable organizational engines:
  1. Lead Generation Machine: Multi-channel customer acquisition system integrating organic content, paid acquisition funnels, and automated WhatsApp nurturing.
  2. Sales Conversion Engine: Consultative closing playbooks, objection response scripts, and pipeline tracking stages that double close rates predictably.
  3. Delivery Excellence Framework: Standardized 48-hour onboarding SOPs, quality assurance checklists, and automated referral/testimonial feedback loops.
  4. Team & Delegation Architecture: 3-Level Delegation Matrix (Task, Process, Outcome) paired with role scorecards and daily sync cadences.
  5. Financial Command Dashboard: CEO Cashflow & Unit Economics Command Center monitoring CAC, LTV, gross margins, and operating runway in 15 minutes weekly.

What is the weekly time commitment required from me as a founder?

The program is specifically engineered for time-constrained business owners. You should budget approximately 3 to 4 hours per week:
  • 60 minutes: Reviewing that week's tactical video module and template assets.
  • 60–90 minutes: Participating in the live weekly Zoom Implementation Lab.
  • 60 minutes: Customizing the week's plug-and-play SOPs or dashboard with your internal team.

What is your implementation-based refund guarantee?

We take all the structural risk through our strict Implementation-Based Double Guarantee:

Complete all 7 module assignments, attend at least 6 of the 8 weekly live strategy sessions, and submit your homework sheets. If you do not successfully install functioning operational machines and see tangible business growth within 60 days, we will either:
  • Continue coaching you 1-on-1 for an additional 30 days at zero extra charge, OR

What if I cannot attend a live weekly Zoom session?

All 8 live implementation calls are recorded in high definition and published to your student portal within 4 hours, featuring detailed topic timestamps, transcript summaries, and resource links. Furthermore, you can submit your workflows or questions in advance via WhatsApp; the coach will audit your submission during the call so you can watch the personalized feedback on replay.

While completing the Business X-Ray™ ($10) provides valuable diagnostic baseline scores, it is not mandatory.
