Part 1 - Program Philosophy & The Integration-First Model
Diagnosis reveals the pattern. Systems stop the bleeding. Integration builds the engine. Tier 3 exists for the HR function that has already been diagnosed (Tier 1) and has installed its core workforce systems (Tier 2), and is now ready for the highest-leverage move of all: integrating technology - AI, HR software and infrastructure - directly into the operation so that HR scale becomes engineered rather than manually forced.
Where the earlier tiers are standardised by design, Tier 3 is deliberately bespoke. No two HR functions carry the same weak dimensions, operate in the same industry, or face the same manual bottleneck. A fixed curriculum would betray the entire diagnostic philosophy. So Tier 3 is built the way a master tailor works - measured first, cut second.

The Three Principles
1. Integration follows the diagnosis. We deploy technology only against dimensions the X-Ray and Rescore proved weak or still manually intensive. No tool is sold for its own sake.
2. Done-WITH-you, not done-for-you. We build it alongside the HR head and their team so capability stays in the organisation after we leave. The client owns the engine.
3. Scope is earned, never assumed. The investment is quoted only after the Tech-Fit Audit. The HR function pays for what fits - nothing more.

Why There Is No Fixed Price or Fixed Timeline
Tier 3 is a consulting-and-implementation engagement, not a course. Its scope, duration and investment are determined entirely by what the diagnosis reveals, what the Debrief installation surfaced, and what the HR function chooses to integrate. A single HR chatbot install is a different engagement from a full HRIS + CRM + AI-agent + workflow-automation rollout. The Tech-Fit Audit (Part 4) is what converts the diagnosis into a precise, custom proposal.

Part 4 - The Tech-Fit Audit (How Custom Scope Is Built)
The Tech-Fit Audit is the entry point to Tier 3. It is a paid 90-minute consultation that carries a separate fee (typically $500-$1,500 USD depending on organisation size), credited against the engagement fee if the client proceeds to Bootcamp scope. If the client does not proceed, the audit output is theirs to keep - a written scope recommendation they can execute internally or with any partner.

The 90-Minute Structure
Time    Segment    Objective
0:00-0:15    Diagnosis Review    Read X-Ray + Rescore aloud; confirm current state
0:15-0:45    Current HR Tech Stack Audit    What is already deployed, integrated, or subscribed
0:45-1:15    Automation Opportunity Mapping    Against the 5 dimensions, what levers apply
1:15-1:30    Prioritisation Discussion    Impact vs effort; sequencing logic

The Six Diagnostic Questions
The audit hinges on six questions the coach asks directly. Answers determine scope. There are no wrong answers - only patterns.
1. Which of the 5 dimensions is still your most manual pain point today, post-Debrief?
2. What percentage of your HR team's week is spent on repetitive Level 1 queries?
3. What HRIS or CRM platform are you already on, and how well-configured is it?
4. What is your realistic annual technology budget - not aspirational, actual?
5. Do you have an internal IT or tech partner who will co-own this deployment, or do we need to bring one?
6. What is the one workforce outcome that, if 10x-improved by technology, would justify this engagement to your CEO?

The Output
Within 5 working days of the audit, the client receives a written 3-page scope recommendation:
- Prioritised list of 3-6 build candidates (from the 5-dimension map)
- Estimated timeline range for each candidate
- Estimated USD investment range for each candidate (individual and combined)
- Explicit list of what is NOT recommended for this engagement (and why)
- One-page executive summary suitable for CEO/CHRO circulation

The client then decides whether to proceed to a full Custom Proposal (Part 6) or to keep the scope recommendation and execute independently.


Part 5 - Growth-Multiplier Mapping & Proposal Logic
Once the audit is complete, the coach converts the prioritised build candidates into a growth-multiplier model that the CEO/CHRO can evaluate financially. This is the difference between "we will build a chatbot" and "we will deploy a chatbot that deflects 40% of Level 1 HR queries, saving [X] HR hours per week, which equals [Y] USD per year in HR capacity redeployed to strategic work."

The Multiplier Categories
Category    What It Measures    Typical Range
Query Deflection    % of Level 1 HR queries handled by bot instead of HR team    30-60% of routine queries
Time Reclaimed    HR team hours per week freed by automation    5-25 hours per week per HR FTE
Onboarding Acceleration    Days shaved off new hire time-to-productivity    3-14 days per new hire
Attrition Prevention    Estimated exit prevention via early-warning AI    2-5 senior exits prevented in Year 1
Recruitment Efficiency    % reduction in Level 1 screening time via voice agent    50-70% reduction in initial screen time
Adoption Rate    % of employees actively using deployed tools within 90 days    60-85% target
Data Quality Uplift    Reduction in data-entry errors via HRIS automation    40-60% reduction

The Proposal Logic
Each build candidate carries an estimated multiplier value. The total proposal aggregates these into a Year 1 impact model:
- Time reclaimed = HR hours per year - HR fully-loaded cost = capacity value in USD
- Attrition prevented = number of exits prevented - fully-loaded exit cost in USD (recruitment + onboarding + productivity loss)
- Adoption target = required condition; if not met, additional support cycle in scope

The proposal is not sold on features. It is sold on the Year 1 multiplier vs the investment. If the multiplier does not clearly exceed the investment by at least 3x in Year 1, the proposal is redesigned - either by removing marginal candidates or by extending the timeline.

Part 7 - The 1-on-1 Strategy & Scoping Call (Full Script) [Hinglish]
How to read this part. Everything in quotation marks is what the trainer SAYS - natural spoken Hinglish, delivered conversationally, not word-for-word. Everything in italics is a stage direction. Bracketed [items] are placeholders to fill live.

Format: Live 1-on-1 call, 90 minutes, paid. Audience: HR head, and optionally CEO/CHRO or IT lead. Goal: Complete the Tech-Fit Audit; land the six diagnostic questions; leave with a mutual understanding of what a Custom Proposal would contain.

Opening & Framing (0:00-0:10)
Open on camera. Warm, senior, unhurried. This is a consultation, not a pitch.
"Namaste. Aaj hum ek Tech-Fit Audit karenge. 90 minutes. Iske end mein aapke paas do cheezein hongi - ek clear samajh ki aapki HR function mein kaunse tech levers actually deploy hone chahiye, aur konsi nahi. Aur haan - koi bhi lever main aaj recommend karunga, uska ek concrete USD number aapko milega. Kya aap ready ho? Chaliye shuru karte hain."

Diagnosis Review (0:10-0:25)

"Sabse pehle, aapki current state. Aapki X-Ray Report mein D2 (Wellbeing) sabse kam thi at 47%, D4 (Growth Gap) 52% par thi. Debrief ke baad Day 60 rescore mein D2 51% par gaya - kuch movement - D4 55% par gaya. Yeh kaunsi picture bataata hai? Systems installed hain, lekin scaling nahi ho paa rahi. Yahaan tech aati hai."

Current HR Tech Stack Audit (0:25-0:45)
Ask specific questions. Take notes on screen.
"Ab aapki current HR tech stack. Ek-ek karke bataayiye - kaunsa HRIS use kar rahe hain? Kaunsa payroll platform? Kya WhatsApp Business API set up hai? Kaunsi tool aapke HR team ke members roz use karte hain vs kaunsi shelfware hai?"

Automation Opportunity Mapping (0:45-1:15)
Now go dimension by dimension. Match manual pain to specific tech levers.

Listen. Then propose specific lever.
"Iske liye eurodigi.ai se ek Wellbeing Pulse Bot deploy ho sakti hai. Weekly voluntary check-in, WhatsApp par. Data anonymised, aggregate dashboard aati hai. Time save - roughly 4-6 HR hours per week. Cost range - $X to $Y depending on scope. Aap iska use kar sakti hai?"

Weave the six diagnostic questions naturally into the dimension conversation. By the end, you should have clear answers to all six.

"Yeh 6 candidates hain. Aap sab kabhi ek saath deploy nahi karengi - yeh classic transformation trap hai. Meri recommendation: teen se shuru karo. Konsi teen? [Client picks based on discussion]. Yeh sequencing hum proposal mein reflect karenge."

Part 9 - Delivery, Governance & Expansion
Delivery Governance
Every Tier 3 engagement has three governance layers:
1. Weekly Delivery Cadence. HR head + tech lead + implementation partner. 30 minutes. Progress, blockers, decisions.
2. Monthly Executive Review. HR head + CEO/CHRO + implementation lead. 45 minutes. Multiplier tracking, adoption metrics, next-month priorities.
3. Quarterly Advisory (Post-Handover). HR head + independent advisor (Euro Digital or Udyog Guru). 60 minutes. Independent second opinion; no sales agenda.

Adoption Discipline
Every deployed technology carries an adoption target (60-85% within 90 days). If adoption is below target at 60 days, the engagement pauses new builds and invests in adoption support. A tool with 30% adoption is worse than no tool.

Expansion Logic
Tier 3 clients typically expand in three directions:
1. Depth expansion - additional levers within a dimension we already deployed (e.g., adding voice agent to a chatbot deployment)
2. Breadth expansion - deploying a new lever category (e.g., adding recruitment agent after HR query bot)
3. Advisory retention - annual advisory contract post the 4 quarterly calls

Expansion is triggered by client outcomes, not by sales calendar. If the client's multiplier is proving out, they typically request expansion themselves.

The Independence Discipline
The most important delivery outcome is client independence. By the end of the engagement, the client's HR + IT team must be able to:
- Operate every deployed tool without external support
- Extend and iterate on chatbot prompts and workflow rules
- Interpret AI dashboard signals and act on them
- Onboard new HR team members to the deployed stack

If any of these are not true at handover, the engagement is not complete. Additional training and support cycles are added at no extra cost until they are.

Quality Standards

- Adoption discipline: no new build starts if the previous build is below 60% adoption at 60 days.
- Independence discipline: engagement is not complete until the client's team can operate, extend, and iterate without us.
- Quarterly Advisory is genuinely independent - no upselling within advisory calls. Expansion opportunities are discussed only when raised by the client.
