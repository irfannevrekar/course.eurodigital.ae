Part 1 - Program Philosophy & The Installation-First Model
A workforce diagnosis changes nothing until it is acted upon. Tier 1 gave the HR head an accurate picture of which of their five workforce dimensions were weak in aggregate. But knowing that Wellbeing (D2) is broken does not build workload governance. Knowing that Growth Gap (D4) is dragging retention does not publish career pathways. The X-Ray Debrief exists to close that gap with a single conviction: clarity becomes workforce transformation only when systems are installed - and most HR leaders will not install them alone.
This program is deliberately NOT another course to watch. It is a 60-day, done-with-you installation engagement. The HR head and their function-head partners bring their organisational X-Ray diagnosis; the coach and curriculum build the missing workforce systems alongside them, session by session, until the organisation runs with predictable people-outcomes and the HR function is no longer the bottleneck.

The Three Delivery Principles
1. Installation over information. Every session ends with a system partly built, not merely a concept understood. Output, not notes.
2. Sequence by aggregate diagnosis, not by syllabus. Each organisation installs systems in the order their X-Ray prioritised - weakest dimension first, highest leverage first.
3. HR + function heads do the reps together; we hold the structure. Done-with-you means the internal team builds their own systems with our templates, scripts and accountability - so the systems stay theirs after day 60.

Position Within the Three-Tier Suite
Tier 2 is the installation engine of the workforce value ladder. It consumes a Tier 1 diagnosis as its input and produces a systemised workforce as its output - which in turn qualifies the organisation for technology-led HR scaling in Tier 3.

Part 4 - The Curriculum: 8 Live Sessions Over 60 Days
The 8-session sequence follows a deliberate rhythm. Sessions 1 and 8 are executive; Sessions 2-7 are system-build sessions. Two of the six build sessions are RED (confidentiality-critical, restricted room). Each session is 2.5 hours except Session 8 which is 90 minutes.

Session    Day    Focus    Room Composition    Confidentiality    Systems Advanced

2    Day 8    Role Clarity (D1)    HR head + 2 lowest-scoring function heads    OPEN    Role Clarity System installed for 2 functions
3    Day 15    Wellbeing Operations (D2)    HR head + COO only    RED    Wellbeing Operations System installed silently
4    Day 22    Focus Culture (D3)    HR head + 1 pilot department head    OPEN    Focus Culture pilot launched
5    Day 30    Career Pathways (D4)    HR head + all function heads    OPEN    3 career pathways published
6    Day 37    Manager Effectiveness (D5)    HR head + 2 HRBPs only    RED    Manager cohort programme launched
7    Day 45    Culture Signals Dashboard    HR head + executive rep + finance partner    OPEN    6 signals + quarterly rhythm calendared
8    Day 60    Consolidation & Executive Readout    Full room - CEO/CHRO + all function heads    OPEN    4-act readout + 3 next-quarter asks + programme graduation

RED session protocol. Sessions 3 and 6 carry confidentiality risks: Session 3 because wellbeing structural fixes reveal workload distribution details that could affect individual managers' standing if leaked; Session 6 because manager effectiveness data patterns can be misused for appraisal without a firewall. RED sessions have a written confidentiality banner, restricted room, no recordings shared outside the room, and a written data-firewall protocol reviewed at the top of the session.

Part 6 - Progress Tracking & The Workforce Health Rescore
Two tracking artefacts run across the 60 days:

1. The System Installation Tracker (Weekly)
A simple RAG dashboard updated every Friday by the HR head, tracking one row per system across five columns: Owner Assigned / Kickoff Completed / Working Asset Delivered / Team Rollout Complete / Sustained at 30 days. The coach reviews this dashboard every Monday and follows up on any RED items.

2. The Workforce Health Rescore (Day 60)
On Day 55-57, a shortened 25-question pulse (5 questions per dimension) is sent to the same employees who participated in the Tier 1 X-Ray. Response rate target: 80% of original participants. The rescore produces:
- Per-dimension movement (baseline vs Day 60)
- Direction of change (GREEN moving well / AMBER movement started but early / RED concerning)
- Function-level breakdown where sample size >=5
- Attrition-risk cluster movement (has it grown or shrunk?)

The rescore is the primary evidence of ROI for the Session 8 executive readout. Realistic expectations: 4-6 metrics GREEN, 1-2 AMBER, 0-1 RED. Full outcome in 60 days is unrealistic and should not be promised. Direction of change is the real success indicator.


Part 7 - The Live Kickoff Session (Full Script) [Hinglish]
How to read this part. Everything in quotation marks is what the trainer SAYS - natural spoken Hinglish, delivered conversationally, not word-for-word. Everything in italics is a stage direction. Bracketed [items] are placeholders to fill live.

Format: Live (in-person preferred, Zoom acceptable), 2.5 hours, one-time. Audience: HR head + CEO or CHRO + all function heads. Goal: Secure written executive mandate, align the 60-day sequence, and set the tone for the entire programme.

Welcome & Room Composition (0:00-0:15)
Open standing, warm and direct. Acknowledge each person by name and role.
"Namaste, aur welcome. Aaj hum ek 60-din ka commitment karne aaye hain - HR nahi, executive team ek saath. Yeh important hai. Kyunki jo systems hum agle do mahine mein install karenge, woh sirf HR ki responsibility nahi ban sakti. Hum yahaan ek shared executive project shuru kar rahe hain, aur is room ka har member is journey mein co-signer hai."
Recognise the diagnostic work already done.
"Aapki organisation ne pehle Workforce X-Ray complete kiya. Aapke employees ne 60 sawaalon ke honest jawaab diye. Aapki HR head ke paas aggregate report hai, aur unko privately unki own HR Mindset report bhi mili. Aaj hum us report ko executive team ke saamne open karenge - aur tay karenge ki 60 din baad kya alag dikhega."

The X-Ray Diagnosis Review (0:15-0:45)
Have the HR head walk through the aggregate report on screen. Coach stays quiet - this is the HR head's moment.
Coach interjects only after the HR head finishes.
"Bahut clear. Ab main teen observations rakhta hoon - simple, structural."
Deliver 3 observations from the pre-read of the report. Format: (1) primary bottleneck naming, (2) secondary + why it compounds primary, (3) attrition risk cluster.
"Observation 1: aapka primary bottleneck [Dimension X] hai. Yeh matlab: [plain-English organisational implication]."
"Observation 2: aapka secondary bottleneck [Dimension Y] hai, aur yeh primary ko compound kar raha hai kyunki [connection]."
"Observation 3: aapke aggregate mein [X] employees CRITICAL band mein hain overall, ya D4 (Growth Gap) par sabse kam score kar rahe hain. Yeh cluster next 6 mahine mein attrition risk carry karta hai - agar hum specific unke saath conversation nahi karte."
Pause. Let it land. Look at the CEO. Do not soften.

The 5-System Roadmap (0:45-1:15)
Present the 8-session sequence and the 5 systems on screen. Frame the sequencing logic clearly.
"Ab 60 din ka sequence dekhiye. Hum saath install nahi karte - hum leverage order mein install karte hain. Aapki diagnosis ke against, hum shuru karenge [Dimension X] se, phir [Dimension Y], phir [Dimension Z]. Sessions 3 aur 6 confidential hongi - main reasoning explain karta hoon."
Explain RED session protocol without being alarming.
"Session 3 mein hum wellbeing structural fixes install karenge - iska matlab hai workload governance, aur woh discussions individual manager ke naam ke saath nahi hone chahiye. Isliye us room mein sirf HR head aur COO hongi. Session 6 mein manager cohort launch karenge - waha bhi confidentiality reason se restricted room hai. Yeh confidentiality integrity ke liye hai, transparency ke against nahi."
Send the room to a 10-min break with a specific instruction.
"Break ke baad ek executive mandate charter draft karenge - jo aap sab sign karenge. Break mein sochiye - aap kaunsi 3 decisions delegate karne ko ready hain HR head ko, 60 din ke liye?"

The Executive Mandate (1:25-2:00)
This is the most important 35 minutes of the session. Slow down. Get every word right.
"Executive Mandate ek written charter hai jo teen cheezein specify karta hai. Ek: HR head ko in 5 systems ko install karne ka full authority hai - har chhoti decision ke liye executive approval nahi chahiye. Do: executive team monthly 30-min standing meeting mein progress review karegi - lekin project management HR head ki hai. Teen: agar koi function head resist kare, HR head executive escalation raste par ja sakti hai, aur executive team support karegi."
Draft the charter live on screen using the template. Fill in specifics with the room's input.
"Ab likhte hain. Konse 5 systems? [list]. Konse 3 decisions HR head khud le sakti hai bina executive approval? [list based on room]. Konsi 2 situations mein executive escalation zaroori hai? [list]. Konsi monthly cadence par review hoga? [date]."
Print or sign digitally at the end. Every attendee signs.
"Yeh charter aaj sign hoga. Yeh symbolic nahi hai - yeh operational hai. Agar aapko lagta hai 60 din baad kuch alag dikhega, toh yeh document hai jo woh possible banata hai."

Q&A & Session 2 Prep (2:00-2:30)
Open the floor. Handle concerns directly. Confirm Session 2 date and pre-work.
"Session 2 ka focus hoga Role Clarity - Dimension 1. Us session mein 2 lowest-scoring function heads honge. Pre-work: aap dono function heads apni current role documentation dekhkar aayenge - jo bhi format mein hai, formal ya informal. Hum us base par charter build karenge. Date: [Day 8]. Time: [time]."
Close with warmth and forward direction.
"Aaj aapne kuch bahut kam organisations ne kiya - aapne ek workforce diagnosis dekhi, imaandaari se accept ki, aur executive team ne 60-din ka structured plan sign kiya. Next 60 din ka kaam humara aur aapka saath hai. Chaliye, install karte hain."

Trainer reminder. The Executive Mandate is the entire product's foundation. Without it, every subsequent session gets derailed by "let me check with leadership." With it, the sequence executes.

Part 9 - The Ascension Bridge to Tier 3
The ascension from Tier 2 to Tier 3 is governed by the rescore outcomes, not by a hard sell. Use the Day-60 rescore profile to match the right message to the right organisation. Tier 3 is bespoke - the Bootcamp has no fixed price; every scope is quoted post-Tech-Fit Audit in USD.
Day-60 Rescore Profile    Recommended Next Step    Messaging Angle
Systems installed but manual-effort-heavy    Tier 3 Bootcamp    "You have installed the systems. Now let us automate them so they scale without proportional HR effort."
Systems installed + specific tech pain named    Tier 3 Bootcamp    "The specific pain you named [e.g. leave query overload, manual onboarding] is a known Tier 3 solve. Let us scope it."
Systems partially installed + still firefighting    Not yet ready for Tier 3    "Complete the installation before adding automation. Any tech deployed on incomplete systems compounds the mess. Consider a Debrief refresh."
Systems installed + rescore movement strong    Tier 3 Bootcamp - full scope    "Your foundation is strong. This is exactly the moment to integrate the technology layer. Book the Tech-Fit Audit."
Systems installed + HR Mindset moved from CRITICAL/CONFLICTED to READY/CHRO    Tier 3 Bootcamp - strategic scope    "You are now operating at CHRO level. Let us discuss a bespoke integration engagement that matches that."

Ethical guardrail. If Tier 3 is not the right fit at Day 60, we say so directly. The downsell is a Debrief refresh (revisit 1-2 systems) or a Rescore+Roadmap engagement six months later. Never pressure. The diagnosis and installation are delivered in full regardless of Tier 3 ascension.
