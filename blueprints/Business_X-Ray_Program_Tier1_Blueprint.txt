Part 1 - Program Philosophy & The Diagnostic-First Model
Every great intervention begins with an accurate diagnosis. A physician who prescribes before examining is guilty of negligence; a business advisor who recommends tactics before understanding the underlying system commits the same error. The Business X-Ray(tm) program is built on a single founding conviction: most businesses are not failing for lack of effort - they are failing for lack of an accurate diagnosis.
This redesign repositions Business X-Ray(tm) from a self-paced information product into a diagnostic-first experience. The learner does not merely consume content; they submit themselves to a structured 62-point examination across seven operational layers and one mindset layer. Only after that examination does teaching begin - and that teaching is calibrated to what the diagnosis reveals.
The Three Pedagogical Principles
1. Diagnosis precedes prescription. No fix is taught until the bottleneck is named. The learner earns clarity before they are offered a cure.
2. Personalisation drives retention. Generic courses are abandoned; a report with the learner's own name and numbers is studied. The masterclass speaks to the cohort's real, aggregated data.
3. Clarity creates the appetite to ascend. When a learner sees precisely what is broken and why, the desire to fix it - with expert help - becomes self-generated rather than sold.

Business X-Ray(tm) is the entry tier of a deliberate value ladder. Each tier is a complete, standalone outcome and simultaneously the qualified gateway to the next. All prices are one-time, in USD, with a lower rate for India and a worldwide rate for all other markets.

Part 3 - The 7-Layer Diagnostic Architecture
The diagnostic instrument examines seven operational layers and one psychological layer. The layers are sequenced deliberately: each rests on the one before it. A business cannot fix sales (Layer 4) while its model (Layer 1) is incoherent, and cannot reclaim the owner's time (Layer 6) until delegation (Layer 5) exists.

The Qualitative Anchors (Open Questions That Power Personalisation)
Eight open prompts give the masterclass its human texture and the report its specificity. These are not scored, but they are the most valuable inputs the coach receives:
• QB5 - the customer transformation, in one sentence.
• QC5 - the actual revenue-stream breakdown by percentage.
• QF5 - the top three tasks the owner knows should be delegated.
• QH5 - the owner's own assessment of the single biggest bottleneck.
• QH6 - what they have already tried, and why it failed.
• QI9 - the limiting belief they suspect is holding them back.
• QI10 / QJ1-QJ2 - the desired 12-month picture and the 90-day income goal.

The Report-Generation Logic (Internal)
From the scored data, the coach generates two short reports using a fixed, repeatable method:
1. Identify the lowest-scoring layer. This is the primary bottleneck and the headline of the Business Fix Report.
2. Identify the second-lowest layer. This is the secondary bottleneck and the second priority.
3. Map each weak layer to its fix module (see the table below) and to any relevant Tier 3 technology lever.
4. Identify the lowest 2-3 mindset items (QI1-QI8) and prescribe the matching shift for the Mindset Fix Report.
5. Cross-reference QH5/QH6 (the owner's self-named bottleneck and past failures) to confirm or gently challenge the data-driven finding.
