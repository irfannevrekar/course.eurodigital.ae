Part 1 - Program Philosophy & The Diagnostic-First Model
Every great intervention begins with an accurate diagnosis. A physician who prescribes before examining is guilty of negligence; an HR leader who launches training, engagement or wellness programs before understanding the underlying workforce system commits the same error. The Workforce X-Ray(tm) program is built on a single founding conviction: most organisations do not have a people problem for lack of investment - they have one for lack of an accurate diagnosis.
This program repositions employee assessment from an annual survey ritual into a diagnostic-first experience. The organisation does not merely receive a satisfaction score; every participating employee submits themselves to a structured 60-question examination across five dimensions of workplace health. Only after that examination does interpretation begin - and that interpretation is calibrated to what the aggregate diagnosis actually reveals about the workforce.

The Three Pedagogical Principles
1. Diagnosis precedes prescription. No fix is proposed until the aggregate bottleneck is named. The HR leader earns clarity before they are offered a cure.
2. Personalisation drives retention on both sides. Generic engagement surveys produce generic dashboards no one reads. A dual-report structure - one confidential report to each employee, one aggregate diagnostic to the organisation - creates two committed readers per participant.
3. Clarity creates the appetite to install. When an HR leader sees precisely which dimension is dragging the workforce down and why, the desire to fix it - with expert help - becomes self-generated rather than sold.

Position Within the Three-Tier Suite
Workforce X-Ray(tm) is the entry tier of a deliberate value ladder. Each tier is a complete, standalone outcome and simultaneously the qualified gateway to the next. All prices are in USD, with a lower rate for India and a worldwide rate for all other markets.

Part 3 - The 5-Dimension Diagnostic Architecture
The diagnostic instrument examines five dimensions of workplace health as experienced by each individual employee, aggregated to reveal the organisational pattern. The dimensions are sequenced deliberately: each rests on the ones before it. An organisation cannot fix career growth (Dimension 4) while performance clarity (Dimension 1) is broken, and cannot lift culture (Dimension 5) until wellbeing and behavioural patterns (Dimensions 2-3) are addressed.
Dimension    Name    What It Measures    Max    Diagnostic Question
Dimension 1    Performance & Capability Signature    Role clarity, capability fit, feedback quality, KPI alignment, recognition    60    Are employees deployed to their strengths, with clear success criteria and the tools to deliver?
Dimension 2    Wellbeing & Stress Load    Sustainable pace, workload governance, recovery capacity, psychological safety    70    Can employees sustain their current pace without burning out - and does the organisation notice when they cannot?
Dimension 3    Behavioural & Productivity Patterns    Focus time, meeting load, deep work capacity, prioritisation discipline    60    Do employees have the operational conditions to do focused, high-quality work?
Dimension 4    Growth Gap & Aspiration Alignment    Career visibility, learning access, promotion pathway, aspiration match    60    Do employees see a professional future here that matches what they actually want?
Dimension 5    Team, Manager & Culture    Manager effectiveness, team dynamics, psychological safety, cultural coherence    50    Do employees experience their manager and team as an accelerator or as friction?

The Sixth Layer - HR Leader Mindset. Scored separately out of 40, the HR Mindset layer is completed by the HR head (not by employees) and assesses the psychological patterns that silently govern how the HR function itself operates: activity substitution vs. outcome focus, executive-pushback anticipation, appraisal capture risk, and confidentiality integrity. A workforce can never outgrow the maturity of its HR function; this is why the diagnosis examines the HR leader's own mental model alongside employee experience.

How the Dimensions Map to the Cure
Each dimension, when weak in aggregate, points to a specific fix taught in Tier 2 and - where technology is the lever - to a specific service deployed in Tier 3. This mapping is what makes the diagnosis actionable rather than merely descriptive.
Weak Dimension    Tier 2 Fix System    Tier 3 Technology Lever (where relevant)
D1: Performance & Capability    Role Clarity System (charters, KPI reset, capability audit)    HRIS / Odoo HR configuration, capability-mapping AI, feedback automation
D2: Wellbeing & Stress Load    Wellbeing Operations System (workload governance, silent structural fixes)    Wellbeing pulse chatbot, workload dashboard, early-warning AI alerts
D3: Behavioural & Productivity    Focus Culture System (calendar culture, deep-work pilot)    Meeting analytics, workflow automation, notification governance tooling
D4: Growth Gap & Aspiration    Career Pathways System (published paths, growth cadence)    Learning platform integration, AI pathway recommendations, LMS deployment
D5: Team, Manager & Culture    Manager Cohort + Culture Signals Dashboard    Conversation intelligence, manager enablement bot, culture signals AI layer


Part 4 - The Workforce X-Ray Form (Diagnostic Engine)
The instrument comprises 60 scored employee questions across 5 dimensions, plus a separate 10-item HR Leader Mindset questionnaire completed once by the HR head. Estimated completion time for employees: 35-40 minutes. Estimated completion for HR Leader Mindset: 8 minutes.
The form's integrity depends on one instruction, which must be repeated at the top of the form and in every delivery message:
"Be honest. No one else sees your raw answers. Your individual answers are seen only by you and your assigned certified assessor. Your employer receives only aggregated, anonymised data across five or more employees. Your data is deleted 180 days after engagement close."

B    Dimension 1 - Performance & Capability Signature    Q1-Q12    12 scored    60
C    Dimension 2 - Wellbeing & Stress Load    Q13-Q26    14 scored    70
D    Dimension 3 - Behavioural & Productivity Patterns    Q27-Q38    12 scored    60
E    Dimension 4 - Growth Gap & Aspiration Alignment    Q39-Q50    12 scored    60

H    HR Leader Mindset (HR head only)    HRM1-HRM10    8 scored + 2 open    40

Total Workforce Health Score: 300 points across the five employee dimensions. HR Leader Mindset Score: 40 points. These two scores are reported separately because they require different prescriptions - one workforce-operational, one HR-function-psychological.

Design integrity check. Before each engagement, verify that scored options render with clean point values (1-5). The instrument must never expose internal scoring notation to the employee mid-question; scores are revealed only in the personal report.

The Qualitative Anchors (Open Questions That Power Personalisation)
Eight open prompts give the masterclass its human texture and the aggregate report its specificity. These are not scored, but they are the most valuable inputs the coach receives:
- The one work moment in the last quarter you felt most alive (per-employee, anonymised in aggregate).
- The single change your manager could make that would meaningfully improve your work (aggregated, thematically clustered).
- The specific capability gap you are aware of but haven't discussed with your manager.
- The one meeting or process you would eliminate if it were your call.
- The career move you actually want in the next 24 months (aggregated by band).
- The wellbeing indicator you would flag if asked confidentially.
- The one thing you would tell the CEO if there were no consequences.
- HRM9 (HR head) - the aspect of the HR function you privately think is under-performing.

The Report-Generation Logic (Internal)
From the scored data, the coach generates three reports per engagement using a fixed, repeatable method:
1. Individual Employee Report - one per participating employee, confidential. Identifies that employee's lowest-scoring dimension (primary personal bottleneck) and second-lowest (secondary), plus a private wellbeing snapshot and 3 personalised recommendations for the next 90 days.
2. Aggregate Organisational Workforce X-Ray Report - to HR head. Identifies the lowest-scoring dimension in aggregate (organisational primary bottleneck), the second-lowest, department-level breakdown where sample size permits (>=5 employees), attrition-risk cluster identification, and prioritised 90-day organisational roadmap.
3. HR Leader Mindset Report - to HR head only, delivered privately alongside the aggregate report. Identifies the 2-3 weakest HR function patterns and prescribes the matching shifts.

Part 10 - Operational Playbook for the Coach/Team

Quality Standards
