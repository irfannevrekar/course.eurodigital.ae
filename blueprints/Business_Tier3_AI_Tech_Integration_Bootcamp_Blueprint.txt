Part 1 - Program Philosophy & The Integration-First Model
Diagnosis reveals the wound. Systems stop the bleeding. Integration builds the engine. Tier 3 exists for the business that has already been diagnosed (Tier 1) and has installed its core systems (Tier 2), and is now ready for the highest-leverage move of all: integrating technology - AI, software and infrastructure - directly into the operation so that growth becomes engineered rather than hoped for.
Where the earlier tiers are standardised by design, Tier 3 is deliberately bespoke. No two businesses carry the same weak layers, operate in the same industry, or face the same constraint. A fixed curriculum would betray the entire diagnostic philosophy. So Tier 3 is built the way a master tailor works - measured first, cut second.
The Three Principles
1. Integration follows the diagnosis. We deploy technology only against layers the X-Ray and Acceleration score proved weak. No tool is sold for its own sake.
2. Done-WITH-you, not done-for-you. We build it alongside the owner and their team so capability stays in the business after we leave. The client owns the engine.
3. Scope is earned, never assumed. The investment is quoted only after the Tech-Fit Audit. The business pays for what fits - nothing more.
Why There Is No Fixed Price or Fixed Timeline
Tier 3 is a consulting-and-implementation engagement, not a course. Its scope, duration and investment are determined entirely by what the diagnosis reveals and what the business chooses to integrate. A single AI chatbot install is a different engagement from a full ERP + CRM + AI-agent + marketing-automation rollout. The Tech-Fit Audit (Part 4) is what converts the diagnosis into a precise, custom proposal.

Part 4 - The Tech-Fit Audit (How Custom Scope Is Built)
The Tech-Fit Audit is Tier 3's diagnostic engine. It is the structured 1-on-1 process that converts everything already known about the business into a precise technology scope. It draws on three inputs and produces one output: a custom proposal.
The Three Inputs
InputWhat It Tells Us1. Business X-Ray diagnosis (Tier 1)Which of the 7 layers scored lowest - the structural weak points and the primary/secondary bottleneck2. Business Acceleration score (Tier 2)Which core systems are now installed and working, and where execution still lags after the 60-day program3. The 1-on-1 discussionIndustry, growth target, budget reality, team capacity, and the owner's own read of the biggest constraint
The Audit Sequence
1. Confirm the weak layers. Reconcile the X-Ray's lowest layers with the Acceleration score and the owner's lived experience. Name the 2-3 layers technology will target first.
2. Map layers to levers. From the Part 3 map, shortlist only the services that fit those layers, the industry, and the team's capacity to adopt them.
3. Model the growth. Attach a realistic multiplier range to each selected lever and sequence them by leverage and dependency.
4. Scope the build. Define phases, timeline and the done-with-you split (what we build, what the team learns to run).
5. Quote. Produce the custom USD investment - services + implementation + any ongoing credits (e.g. AI calling minutes, hosting, subscriptions).

Scoping discipline. If a lever does not map to a proven weak layer, it does not enter the proposal - however impressive the technology. The diagnosis governs the scope; the scope governs the price. This is what keeps Tier 3 honest and the ROI defensible.

The proposal always leads with the gap's cost, then the fix, then the multiplier.

Part 9 - Delivery, Governance & Expansion
As the top tier, Tier 3's reputation is built in delivery, not the sale. Governance keeps the engagement on scope, on time, and provably effective.
Delivery Governance
• Phase gates. Each phase has a defined deliverable and a sign-off before the next begins - no scope creep, no surprise bills.
• Weekly rhythm. A standing check-in per active phase: progress, blockers, and the metric the phase is meant to move.
• Capability transfer. Every build includes documentation and a team walkthrough - done-with-you means they can run it without us.
• BI dashboards. Live KPI dashboards (eurodigi.ai analytics) make the growth multiplier visible and the ROI undeniable.

The Expansion Path
TriggerNext StepAnglePhase set delivered, KPIs upNext-layer integration"Phase 1 moved [metric]. Governance guardrail. Expansion is offered only when the prior phase has demonstrably moved its target metric. We earn the next engagement with results, never with pressure. A proven multiplier is the only pitch Tier 3 needs.

Part 10 - Operational Playbook for the Consulting/Delivery Team
Per-Engagement Checklist
1. Qualify: confirm X-Ray + Acceleration score on file; verify the business is past firefighting.
2. Run the Tech-Fit Audit (Part 7 script); capture weak layers, industry, target, budget reality.
3. Build the proposal using the Part 5 logic; map only fitting levers; sequence by leverage.
4. Quote the custom USD investment; separate one-time build from ongoing credits.
5. On sign-off: kickoff, access checklist, project plan, success metrics per phase.
6. Deliver phase-by-phase with sign-off gates; document and train at each step.
7. Stand up BI dashboards; review the target metric weekly.
8. On proven results, offer the next-layer expansion or a managed retainer.

Quality Standards
• Never propose a lever that doesn't map to a proven weak layer - discipline is the product.
• Never quote before the Tech-Fit Audit; the scope governs the price, not the reverse.
• Always done-with-you: capability must remain in the client's business.
• Tie every phase to a measurable metric; if it can't be measured, it can't be claimed.
• Honour data security & compliance on every AI deployment (encrypted handling, auditable logs).
