Part 1 - Program Philosophy & The Installation-First Model
A diagnosis changes nothing until it is acted upon. Tier 1 gave the owner an accurate picture of which of their seven business layers were weak. But knowing the lead system is broken does not build a lead system. The Business Acceleration Engine exists to close that gap with a single conviction: clarity becomes growth only when systems are installed - and most owners will not install them alone.
This program is deliberately NOT another course to watch. It is a 60-day, done-with-you installation engagement. The owner brings their X-Ray diagnosis; the coach and curriculum build the missing systems alongside them, week by week, until the business runs predictably without the owner as the bottleneck.
The Three Delivery Principles
1. Installation over information. Every session ends with a system partly built, not merely a concept understood. Output, not notes.
2. Sequence by leverage, not by syllabus. Each owner installs systems in the order their X-Ray prioritised - weakest, highest-leverage layer first.
3. The owner does the reps; we hold the structure. Done-with-you means the owner builds their own assets with our templates, scripts and accountability - so the systems stay theirs after day 60.
Position Within the Three-Tier Suite
Tier 2 is the installation engine of the value ladder. It consumes a Tier 1 diagnosis as its input and produces a systemised business as its output - which in turn qualifies the owner for technology-led scaling in Tier 3.

Session Anatomy (Every Session Follows This Arc)

3. Build live (40 min): owners build their own asset on the call, screens shared.

5. Mindset close (5 min): one shift from the X-Ray mindset map, installed as a habit.




The Five Systems in Detail
S1 - The Business Blueprint (Sessions 1-2)
Define the irresistible offer, the ideal customer, and value-based pricing. Output: a one-page Business Blueprint the owner can state in one sentence. Repairs Layers 1 and 2.
S2 - The Lead Generation Machine (Sessions 3-4)
Choose one primary channel, build the offer-to-market message, and stand up lead capture + a simple tracking sheet. Output: a running lead source with measured cost-per-lead. Repairs Layer 3.
S3 - The Sales Conversion System (Session 5)
Install a repeatable sales conversation, a multi-touch follow-up cadence, and a basic CRM pipeline. Output: every lead now moves through a defined process. Repairs Layer 4.
S4 - The Team & Delegation System (Session 6)
Write the three highest-leverage SOPs and execute one real delegation using the task-process-outcome ladder. Output: the owner's calendar gets its first hours back. Repairs Layer 5.
S5 - The CEO Operating System (Session 7)
Build a five-number financial dashboard and a weekly CEO calendar/rhythm so the owner leads from data, not firefighting. Output: visibility and a repeatable weekly operating cadence. Repairs Layers 6 and 7.

Part 10 - Operational Playbook for the Coach/Team

2. Collect each owner's X-Ray; set their personal install order before Kickoff.
3. Run 8 live build sessions; ensure each owner produces the session asset.

Quality Standards
