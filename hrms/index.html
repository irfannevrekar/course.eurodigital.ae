Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about navigating the Human Resource Management System suite.

How do the three tiers of the Human Resource Management System connect?

Our suite follows an interlocking 3-stage organizational pathway:
  • Tier 01 (Workforce X-Ray™): Diagnoses organizational health across 5 dimensions, generating actionable heatmaps and attrition canvases.
  • Tier 02 (Workforce Acceleration Engine™): Installs 5 core HR machines (Role clarity, feedback cadence, manager enablement) over 60 days.
  • Tier 03 (AI + Tech Workforce Bootcamp): Automates routine HR operations (24/7 policy bots, applicant screening, HRIS integration) to scale capacity.

How is employee confidentiality and data security guaranteed?

We implement a strict 5+ Employee Aggregation Rule: no department or group with fewer than 5 respondents can be isolated or inspected individually. Individual responses are encrypted, anonymized, and never shown to leadership in isolation. Raw individual response records are automatically purged after 180 days.

How much time do company leaders and HR need to invest?

In Tier 1, employees spend 15–20 minutes on the diagnostic, while the HR Head and CEO attend a single 2-hour live masterclass. In Tier 2, the HR Head spends ~2.5 to 3.5 hours per week in Done-With-You build sessions, while department heads participate only in designated open sessions, keeping executive disruption minimal.

What regional compliance standards do your HR technology platforms satisfy?

Our technology platforms (eurodigital.ae and eurodigi.ai) strictly adhere to regional privacy and employment governance laws, including the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45/2021), India Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), and GDPR principles, ensuring full regional compliance.
