Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our bespoke AI + Tech Business Integration Bootcamp deployment.

Why is pricing for the AI + Tech Bootcamp custom rather than a fixed fee?

Tier 3 is a custom, Done-With-You technical implementation engineered specifically around your company's operational bottlenecks, database infrastructure, and legacy software stacks.

Because scope varies significantly—from integrating an autonomous WhatsApp lead qualifier to deploying an entire bespoke ERP and multi-agent AI pipeline—exact pricing and timelines are determined transparently during your 1-on-1 Tech-Fit Audit before any commitment is made.

What are the two technology platforms deployed in this bootcamp?

We bridge your operational strategy with our proven two-platform technology ecosystem:
  • eurodigital.ae (Enterprise Software Infrastructure): Core business management applications covering CRM pipelines, custom ERP modules, inventory & project operations, automated billing, and financial analytics.
  • eurodigi.ai (Autonomous AI Systems): Custom AI agents tailored to your domain—including 24/7 inbound WhatsApp lead qualification, automated multi-channel client support, AI document extraction, and predictive business dashboards.

What are the criteria to qualify for this program?

Because this program entails intensive direct technical engineering from our senior software team, enrollment is strictly selective:
  • You must have a completed Business X-Ray™ diagnosis or Business Acceleration Engine™ score on file.
  • Your business generates at least $200K in annual revenue and has moved beyond survival mode.
  • You have an internal team champion designated to collaborate during technical sprints and user testing.
  • You complete the 1-on-1 Tech-Fit Audit strategy call to validate technical feasibility and ROI.

Does your team build everything for us or do we need to hire developers?

You do not need to hire software developers or prompt engineers. Our technical integration team handles the heavy lifting: API integrations, database hooks, AI fine-tuning, workflow scripts, and UI customization. We install the systems alongside your team on a Done-With-You basis, ensuring your operational staff is thoroughly trained to manage and modify the systems after handover.

How long does a typical AI + Tech integration take?

Implementation timelines range from 4 to 12 weeks, structured into three distinct execution phases:
  • Weeks 1–2: Architectural Mapping, Security Protocols & Environment Setup.
  • Weeks 3–8: Software Deployment, API Hooks, AI Agent Training & Staging Sandbox Testing.
  • Weeks 9–12: Production Cutover, Team Enablement Workshops & 30-Day Stabilization Support.

How do you ensure enterprise data security and IP confidentiality?

Data security is our non-negotiable priority. All integrations are governed by comprehensive non-disclosure agreements (NDAs). We implement AES-256 end-to-end encryption at rest and in transit, strict role-based access control (RBAC), and private cloud tenant isolation. Your proprietary business workflows and client data are never used to train public language models.
