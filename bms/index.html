Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about navigating the Business Management System value ladder.

How do the three tiers of the Business Management System work together?

Our suite follows a disciplined Diagnostic-First Architecture:
  • Tier 01 (Business X-Ray™): Uncovers the exact 7-layer bottlenecks, providing total clarity and a 60-day roadmap for just $10.
  • Tier 02 (Business Acceleration Engine™): Installs the 5 core business machines over 60 days on a Done-With-You basis.
  • Tier 03 (AI + Tech Bootcamp): Integrates custom software, CRM/ERP workflows, and autonomous AI agents to scale your proven machines.

Which tier should I start with?

We strongly recommend starting with the Business X-Ray™ ($10). Because it is priced as a friction-free handshake offer, it delivers immediate clarity on which operational layer is bleeding the most cash or founder energy before you invest in heavy execution or software tools.

Can I enroll directly into Tier 2 or Tier 3 without doing Tier 1?

Yes for Tier 2: Founders generating $100K+ annual revenue who already recognize their operational bottlenecks can join the Business Acceleration Engine™ directly.

For Tier 3 (AI + Tech Bootcamp), an operational diagnostic (either Tier 1 or Tier 2) is required on file so our engineering team can scope technical levers accurately against verified operational baselines.

What guarantees protect my investment across these courses?

We stand 100% behind our methodologies:
  • Tier 1: 100% No-Questions-Asked Money-Back Guarantee within 7 days.
  • Tier 2: Implementation-Based Double Guarantee (60 days to functioning machines, or 30 days free 1-on-1 coaching or 100% refund).
  • Tier 3: Minimum 3x Year-1 Projected Financial ROI Hurdle before project approval.
