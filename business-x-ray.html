Core Philosophy

The Diagnostic-First Model

A physician who prescribes before examining is guilty of negligence; a business advisor who recommends tactics before understanding the underlying system commits the same error.

Principle 01

Diagnosis Precedes Prescription

Most businesses fail not for lack of effort, but for lack of accurate diagnosis. No fix is recommended until the primary bottleneck is identified and named.

Principle 02

Personalisation Drives Retention

Generic business courses are abandoned. A personal report with the founder's own name and actual layer scores is studied and executed.

Principle 03

Clarity Creates Appetite to Scale

When an owner sees precisely what operational layer is dragging profits down, the desire to fix it becomes self-generated rather than sold.

Diagnostic Instrument

The 7-Layer Diagnostic Architecture

62 questions across 7 operational layers (210 points) + Founder Mindset layer (40 points). Total: 250 points.

Layer 01 Max: 30 Pts

Offer & Value Proposition

Positioning, pricing power, target audience clarity, value messaging, and differentiation margin.

Layer 02 Max: 30 Pts

Lead Generation & Acquisition

Lead predictability, multi-channel acquisition, customer acquisition cost (CAC) control, and organic vs paid split.

Layer 03 Max: 30 Pts

Sales Conversion & Pipeline

Sales scripts, objection handling, closing rate, CRM pipeline tracking, and follow-up discipline.

Layer 04 Max: 30 Pts

Service Delivery & Operations

Onboarding speed, quality control, SOP documentation, fulfillment efficiency, and operational capacity.

Layer 05 Max: 30 Pts

Retention, LTV & Referral Engine

Churn rate, repeat purchases, client satisfaction, referral mechanics, and lifetime value optimization.

Layer 06 Max: 30 Pts

Team, Delegation & Leadership

Org chart clarity, key-person dependency, delegation framework, role clarity, and team accountability.

Layer 07 Max: 30 Pts

Financial Management & Cashflow

Margin health, cash flow visibility, unit economics, profit allocation, and financial forecasting.

The 8th Layer Max: 40 Pts

Founder Mindset Layer

Completed by the founder. Evaluates Activity Default, Bottleneck Attachment, Decision Avoidance, Micromanagement, Financial Avoidance, & Comfort-Zone Traps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Business X-Ray™ diagnostic protocol, deliverables, and guarantee.

How is the Business X-Ray™ different from ordinary business courses or coaching?

Unlike generic 40-hour lecture libraries or theory-heavy coaching programs that you watch passively, the Business X-Ray™ is an active diagnostic intervention:
  • 62-Point Diagnostic Assessment: You audit all 7 operational and mindset layers of your enterprise (Strategy, Marketing, Sales, Operations, People, Finance, and Leadership).
  • Coach-Reviewed Business & Mindset Fix Report: You receive a custom diagnostic report highlighting your exact operational leakage points and high-ROI levers.
  • Live 2-Hour Interactive Masterclass: Our systems consultant audits your scores live, walks through fixes, and helps you build a 60-day prioritised execution roadmap.

Why is the Business X-Ray™ priced at just $10? What's the catch?

There is no catch, no hidden recurring subscription, and no locked content. We intentionally price the diagnostic at a friction-free entry point ($10) because our core principle is that diagnosis must always precede prescription.

By delivering exceptional clarity in your personalized Diagnostic Report and live masterclass, we earn your trust. Many founders subsequently choose to enroll in our 60-day Business Acceleration Engine™ or AI Bootcamp, but the Business X-Ray™ stands entirely on its own as a complete, high-value asset.

What industries and business models does this diagnostic work for?

The 7-layer diagnostic framework evaluates fundamental operational systems that govern every profitable business model:
  • B2B & B2C Service Agencies, Consultancies & Professional Firms
  • Manufacturing, Distribution, Engineering & Industrial Units
  • Retail, Wholesale, Trading & E-Commerce Operations
  • Coaching, Education, Training & Information Businesses
  • Healthcare, Contracting, Real Estate & Tech Enterprises
Whether you generate $150K or $15M annually, the primary bottlenecks invariably sit in one of the 7 foundational layers.

How long does the diagnosis take and what is the total time commitment?

The total time commitment is approximately 3 hours:
  • 35 to 45 minutes to complete the 62-point self-diagnostic audit and submit your current operational metrics.
  • 2 hours to attend the live, interactive Zoom Diagnostic Masterclass where we debrief scores, unpack bottlenecks, and formulate your 60-day action plan.

Yes. Every live masterclass is recorded in full HD and uploaded to your private member portal within 4 hours. You receive lifetime access to the video recording, transcript summaries, downloadable worksheets, and Q&A timestamps. You can also submit specific diagnostic questions ahead of time to be answered during the live call.
