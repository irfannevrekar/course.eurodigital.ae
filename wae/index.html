Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our 60-Day Done-With-You Workforce Acceleration Engine™ implementation.

What are the 5 core workforce systems installed over the 60 days?

Over the 8-week installation cadence, we co-build and install five resilient HR machines:
  1. Role Architecture & Accountability Framework: Eliminates cross-functional overlap via outcome-driven job scorecards, RACI matrices, and decision rights.
  2. Performance Cadence Engine: Replaces obsolete annual reviews with continuous 90-day review cycles, weekly 1-on-1 sprint templates, and clear merit rubrics.
  3. Talent Pipeline & Internal Mobility Architecture: Maps bench strength for critical single-point dependencies and defines transparent career pathways.
  4. Manager Enablement Machine: Equips frontline managers with plug-and-play coaching frameworks, difficult conversation playbooks, and delegation audits.
  5. Workforce Intelligence Command Center: Deploys a live HR metrics cockpit tracking leading attrition indicators, eNPS, and retention health in 15 minutes weekly.

What does "Done-With-You" delivery look like for our HR team?

Rather than delivering generic advice or classroom lectures, our certified assessors work side-by-side with your leadership. In 8 weekly live 90-minute Zoom implementation labs, we take your company's actual org charts, department structures, and compensation bands and co-create finalized, ready-to-deploy SOPs and dashboards. Your HR team leaves each session with implemented operating tools, not just theory.

What are RED banner sessions and how do they handle sensitive issues?

Sessions 2, 4, and 7 are designated RED Banner Sessions. These closed-door strategic working sessions are strictly limited to the HR Head, CEO/COO, and the lead assessor. Highly sensitive topics—such as executive performance misalignment, confidential restructuring, retention lock-ins for flight-risk leaders, and sensitive compensation bands—are analyzed and solved with complete organizational confidentiality.

How do we measure tangible improvement after 60 days?

At the conclusion of the program, we administer the formal Day-60 Workforce Health Rescore. We re-audit all 5 dimensions across your organization to quantify concrete movement against your baseline diagnostic data—measuring percentage increases in role clarity, reduction in high-performer flight risk, and elevation in managerial coaching frequency.

What is the weekly time commitment for the HR team and function heads?

The program is designed to integrate smoothly into active business operations:
  • HR Head / People Lead: Approximately 2.5 to 3.5 hours per week (90-minute live working session + 60 minutes internal workflow calibration).
  • Business Unit / Function Heads: Participate only in select open sessions (Sessions 1, 3, 5, and 8) for 60–90 minutes, preserving executive bandwidth.
  • CEO / COO: 60 minutes in RED Banner strategy sessions and the final executive readout.

What ongoing support is provided after Day 60 graduation?

Graduation includes 4 weeks of post-program WhatsApp and email advisory support directly with your lead assessor to assist with rollouts and team adoption. You also retain lifetime access to our cloud repository of HR SOPs, job descriptions, evaluation matrices, and quarterly dashboard audit templates.
