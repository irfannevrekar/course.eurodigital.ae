The 3-Minute Business Clarity Test

Unsure which operational system is bleeding the most revenue? Answer 3 quick structural questions to get an instant personalized diagnosis and recommended roadmap.

Question 1: What is your primary business bottleneck right now?

Be completely honest—where are you spending most of your fire-fighting energy?

"I have no structure or clarity. Leads are random, and I'm guessing what to do next."
"I generate leads but conversion is manual, slow, or inconsistent. I lack predictable sales systems."
"I am trapped in daily operations. My team is completely dependent on me, and I work 12+ hour days."

Question 2: If you take a 3-week complete holiday today, what happens?

This is the ultimate test of operational systems vs. founder hustle.

"Total operational and revenue collapse within 48 hours."
"The business survives, but chaos/fires will break out and clients will complain."
"Everything runs predictably. The team manages outcomes, and revenue keeps flowing."

Question 3: What is your current annual business revenue range?

Selecting your bracket helps pinpoint the exact operational phase you are currently in.

"Under ₹10 Lakhs (Startup / Self-employed scale)"
"₹10 Lakhs - ₹50 Lakhs (Growing business needing systems)"
"₹50 Lakhs - ₹5 Crores (Established scale needing executive shift)"
